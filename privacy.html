Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. About Maya

Maya is a personal AI assistant developed for private, non-commercial use.

2. Google Account Access

Maya requests access to Google Gmail data only with authorization from the Google account owner. The application may read, organize, and modify email messages as required for its authorized personal workflows.

3. Data Processing and Storage

Maya may process email content and metadata to carry out user-authorized tasks. Data may be stored locally on the user's computer. Processing may involve external AI services, depending on the configured workflow.

4. Data Sharing

Maya is not intended for commercial distribution. Google account data is not sold or used for advertising. Data may be transmitted to service providers when necessary for authorized functionality.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored application data may be retained for operational purposes. The account owner can discontinue use, revoke Google account access, and request deletion of locally stored application data.

6. Security

Maya is designed to restrict access to authorized accounts and to protect stored credentials.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data handling practices change.
